# Ending and item map

## Equipment

- **cassette — Elian’s cassette:** Two bells after a gull cry. “The sea is the long breathing. If I answer with your name, it is not me.” The leader bears your sister’s handwriting.
- **key — Intake key:** An iron key stamped VISITOR. The reverse is deliberately blank.
- **lantern — Service lantern:** A cold lantern from the market. Its empty reservoir takes the resin treatment described at the apothecary.
- **resin — Tested resin:** The blue-sealed mixture burns without smoke. Combine it with the service lantern.
- **protectedLantern — Treated lantern:** A steady amber flame. It slows exposure in the lower passages; it cannot make a voice truthful.
- **register — First Register:** Two independent records verify June Arlen, Tomas Reed, Ada Wren and Niko Bell. Chosen aliases remain beside their names.
- **archive — Original shutoff order:** At 02:13 the oxygen supply was isolated. The signature and carbon impression match. This is physical evidence, not a voice.
- **relay — Relay contract:** Orison Recovery will carry the acoustic signal through municipal infrastructure. A transmission channel can also carry recorded testimony.
- **chalk — Survey chalk:** Waterproof chalk supplied with a return-route diagram. Leave marks at physical junctions; do not follow voices.
- **core — Bell Core:** The portable resonator contains a pattern formed from the erased records. It is a vessel, not another child.

## Endings

### witness: THE WITNESS

A record belongs to everyone.

You send the shutoff order, the relay contract and eight verified names through the town’s public line. You leave each chosen alias beside the name an institution gave it. The bell tries to add a thirteenth child. You identify the vessel instead. With the truth held in many places, it no longer needs one body to carry it. At the road, the sea makes an ordinary sound. You do not ask it to carry another name.

### silence: THE SILENCE

An ending is not an erasure.

Outer, inner, outer. The final brace breaks beneath your hand. The voices are allowed to stop. No new name replaces them. Saint Orison loses its impossible rooms, but the papers you carry do not prove everything that happened here. You leave the unproven spaces blank. This time, a blank is an admission of what you do not know—not an instruction to forget.

### vessel: THE OFFERED NAME

A burden has changed hands.

You give the machine your own copied pattern. The town grows quiet, and the others can leave, but the bell still has a container. A person at the gate recognizes your face. Your name will not stay with them. You do not ask remembrance as payment. Behind you, a new line appears in the visitor ledger. Its ink is still wet.

The ending is selected at the Well and confirmed before departure. The following rooms express it; optional relationship observations remain distinct. All three endings have legal command routes. Branch tests cover each immediate alternative, not every combinatorial playthrough.
