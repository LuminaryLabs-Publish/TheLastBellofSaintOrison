# Exit Road: scene writing

## Arrival

The gatehouse fan turns in the morning air. The fourth roster line is still blank unless you offered your pattern below. You stop where the cassette first played. The sea sounds larger now that nothing is trying to speak over it.

## The original cassette

Elian: “The long breathing.” You let the recording reach its ordinary click at the end. An old message does not need to answer a new question.

## Look back at Saint Orison

The tower is a structure again. Some windows are broken, some are lit, and people will have to decide what to do with both. Your intervention changed the town. It did not replace the work of living in it.

## Write the first line home

A blank page rests against your satchel. You can begin without pretending the account is complete.

## Wait for Elian

You leave space beside you without calling a name into the gate. What she remembers depends on your intervention. What you owe her does not.

## Completion

You write one honest sentence. The road ahead is ordinary, and that is enough to begin.

## Departures

### Leave Saint Orison

You walk toward the sea road with the record and its consequences.

### Stay until the record is safely copied

You delay your departure for an ordinary responsibility: ensuring the papers can outlast you.
