# Market Street: scene writing

## Arrival

Rain drums on canvas roofs. An abandoned freight lantern lies beside a half-lowered shutter. The café’s cable disappears through a service trunk on the far side. This crossing can be left safe for someone returning behind you.

## Freight safety plate

PIN THE WEIGHT. RAISE THE SHUTTER. ENGAGE THE CATCH. The plate shows a raised shutter held by the catch, never by a person beneath it.

## Abandoned service lantern

The lantern is cold but intact. A paper tie reads: “Apothecary resin only. Smoke carries the echo.” You put it in your satchel.

## Shutter winch

The winch has a weight pin, a winding handle and a locking catch. All three are within reach from the safe side.

## Repair stall

A bent return-route sign lies beneath a tarpaulin. You straighten it and point it toward the safe freight alcove. Somebody following you will not have to guess.

## Completion

The catch locks with a clear metallic click. The crossing remains open without your weight on the handle.

## Departures

### Cross through the freight opening

The lantern knocks softly against your satchel.

### Secure a return marker before crossing

You tie a pale marker at the opening. The route will be recognizable even without the lamps.
