# Town Square: scene writing

## Arrival

The town square is quiet enough to hear the sea. A few residents wait beside the public notice stand. They ask what happened beneath their homes. You have knowledge, physical evidence and gaps. None of those should be disguised as another.

## Public notice stand

The evacuation notice calls the hospital incident a structural failure. There is room beside it for a record that can be inspected, challenged and kept by more than one person.

## Review the evidence

You separate what you witnessed, what was independently recorded, what Elian told you and what remains unknown. The distinction belongs in the public account.

## Prepare your account

The residents are listening. You can give them a truthful account without turning uncertainty into a performance.

## Father Vale at the fountain

Vale: “Will they forgive us?” Mara: “That is not a task I can complete for you.” You leave him with his signed confession and a choice to repeat it publicly.

## Completion

You lay out the records and label their limits. The residents can keep copies. No single person has to become the town’s memory again.

## Departures

### Leave copies with the residents

The record begins to exist in more than one place.

### Arrange independent custody of the originals

You identify which papers are originals and which are copies. The residents choose separate custodians.
