# Return Ascent: scene writing

## Arrival

The pipes have stopped singing together. Some lights are gone, but the material route still exists. What you repaired, marked or broke now matters. Elian is beside you only if you opened the isolation door; otherwise the archive line speaks from fixed emergency stations.

## Return survey plate

The reverse-route instruction reads: EMPTY CIRCLE, CLOSED EYE, MOUTH, OPEN EYE. Even if you left no chalk, the embossed symbols remain. Familiar voices are not a substitute for a physical route.

## Emergency line

Elian: “I can follow the same physical route. Tell me the symbols, not what the voices say.” You explain each junction. If she stayed at the archive station, this line is her way toward the protected upper stair.

## Return gates

The selectors open the route in reverse. Their raised symbols can be checked by the lantern or by touch.

## Keep the emergency line open

You hold the local relay long enough for Elian to repeat the return instructions. She chooses her own pace. Helping does not erase the records already secured in your satchel.

## Completion

The upper gate releases. You reach the parish stair with the core’s consequence behind you and your evidence still accounted for.

## Departures

### Continue to the square

You take the upper stair. Elian’s route follows the instructions you gave.

### Hold the line until Elian confirms her route

“Open eye,” she says at the last junction. This time the answer comes from a person who can leave.
