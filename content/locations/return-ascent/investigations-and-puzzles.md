# Return Ascent: investigation contract

## Entry requirements

Enter through Well Chamber. Essential clue requirements are `return-order`, `elian-return`.

## Concrete player route

1. Inspect **Return survey plate**. Read its actual record in the scene script. The journal receives `return-order`.
2. Inspect **Emergency line**. Read its actual record in the scene script. The journal receives `elian-return`.

Operate **Return gates** after those records are available.

**Reverse the survey:** Choose the reverse of the descent route.

Available controls: Open eye / Mouth / Closed eye / Empty circle.

Exact solution: **Empty circle → Closed eye → Mouth → Open eye**.

Partial correct answers stay in the current controls. A wrong input clears that attempt and adds up to 12 seconds of exposure within the room's nonnegative limit; it never destroys evidence. Closing/reopening controls restarts an unfinished attempt. Reading the journal freezes pressure; operating controls does not.

Wrong response: The selected passage returns to the pump niche. The raised reverse-route plate remains readable.

Success response: The upper gate releases. You reach the parish stair with the core’s consequence behind you and your evidence still accounted for.

Success commits solved state; evidence rewards are described by observations and departures.

No additional exit inventory condition is imposed by this room.

## Optional investigations

- **Keep the emergency line open:** You hold the local relay long enough for Elian to repeat the return instructions. She chooses her own pace. Helping does not erase the records already secured in your satchel.

## Hint ladder

1. The return plate reverses the descent.
2. Begin at the empty circle.
3. Empty circle, closed eye, mouth, open eye.

## Acceptance

Early operation must refuse missing records. Wrong inputs must remain recoverable. Repeated inspection or solved operation must not duplicate rewards. Successful operation must finish the Sequence's operate node; a departure without that state is rejected.
