# Flooded Archive: pressure and outcomes

Base continuous exposure limit: **135 seconds**. This is the current loop parameter, not a measured location duration.

Warning above 60%: Water reaches the first red mark on the shelf legs.

Critical above 85%: The far shelf tilts. The raised exit is still clear.

Voluntary or forced retreat: You return to the raised index desk. The pump’s reserve tank drains the nearest aisle; essential records remain in their protective cases.

Retreat resets local exposure, increments the setback count and preserves essential evidence. Menus, reading and application suspension freeze exposure. Puzzle controls remain active. Gentle multiplies rate by 0.5; Story makes it zero. A treated lantern multiplies it by 0.8. The retained relay slows School/Archive exposure by 0.9; marked tunnels and stabilized market routes reduce Return Ascent exposure. These are rules in the game Kit, not animation timing.
