# Visitor Centre: scene writing

## Arrival

The visitor centre smells of warm paper. Every chair faces the illuminated town map. An evacuation notice has yesterday’s date, but the visitor book was signed this morning. Elian has drawn a small wave beside her entry. She did not leave by the road.

## Circuit diagram

The map transformer is isolated from the building supply. The diagram reads: disconnect RED, connect BLUE, then connect AMBER. The small circuit powers only the map. You will not touch the building mains.

## Visitor book

ELIAN VENN — CAFÉ, 07:10. Her note reads: “They sent everyone home except the people who remembered.” Beneath it, the clerk has stamped TRANSFERRED instead of DEPARTED.

## Map service cabinet

Three low-voltage toggles sit inside an insulated enclosure. Their symbols match the circuit diagram.

## Staff evacuation list

Two columns share the same names: DEPARTED and RETAINED. The carbon sheet reveals a deliberate change, not a clerical error. You photograph both layers.

## Completion

The map lights in sections. A chalk line links the Café, market and bell tower. Beside the hospital, an underground route has been rubbed out.

## Departures

### Follow Elian’s café entry

You take the street named in the visitor book.

### Follow the evacuation staff route

The route passes the same café, behind its delivery door. The evacuation list stays with your evidence.
