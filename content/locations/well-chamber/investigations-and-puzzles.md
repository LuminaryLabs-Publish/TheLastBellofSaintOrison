# Well Chamber: investigation contract

## Entry requirements

Enter through Maintenance Tunnels. Essential clue requirements are `names-second-four`, `ending-methods`.

## Concrete player route

1. Inspect **Paired identity records**. Read its actual record in the scene script. The journal receives `names-second-four`.
2. Inspect **Intervention instructions**. Read its actual record in the scene script. The journal receives `ending-methods`.

Operate **Core release collars** after those records are available.

**Release the resonator:** Follow the core’s isolation plate.

Available controls: Close shutter / Isolate feed / Release collars.

Exact solution: **Close shutter → Isolate feed → Release collars**.

Partial correct answers stay in the current controls. A wrong input clears that attempt and adds up to 12 seconds of exposure within the room's nonnegative limit; it never destroys evidence. Closing/reopening controls restarts an unfinished attempt. Reading the journal freezes pressure; operating controls does not.

Wrong response: A mechanical interlock holds. The core remains seated. Begin by closing the acoustic shutter.

Success response: The Bell Core comes free. Eight identities are verified. Choose an intervention with its stated requirements and consequence. Nothing is committed until you confirm.

Success grants `core` exactly once.

No additional exit inventory condition is imposed by this room.

## Acceptance

Early operation must refuse missing records. Wrong inputs must remain recoverable. Repeated inspection or solved operation must not duplicate rewards. Successful operation must finish the Sequence's operate node; a departure without that state is rejected.
