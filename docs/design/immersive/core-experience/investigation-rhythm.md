# Investigation Rhythm

**Target specification; runtime implementation pending.**

Target each active investigation at 1–3 minutes, excluding optional lore and deliberate accessibility pauses. A location can contain several encounters and quiet exploration. Do not claim three hours by multiplying fixed durations.

Start with a readable goal and a safe demonstration, then allow manipulation under the location’s pressure budget. End with a visible consequence and a moment to understand the changed room before the next threat. Avoid stacking a new timer immediately after a long required dialogue.

Adjacent rooms should change the activity: Gate observes; Centre reconnects; Café compares sound; Market handles load; Apothecary tests material; Tower manipulates and decides; School corroborates; Archive recovers; Chapel verifies and chooses. The final two locations reduce urgency and resolve responsibility.

Optional detours need a visible reason to pursue them and a clear route back. Record active manipulation, search, conversation, traversal and recovery separately in the duration worksheet. Overlapping audio and movement count once, not twice.
