# Information and Reading Budget

**Target specification; runtime implementation pending.**

Target ordinary required feedback at one sentence of roughly 4–12 words, and a visible objective at roughly 8–16 words. These are editing targets, not truncation rules. Consequence previews may use up to about 35 words plus exact prerequisite labels when needed for informed choice. Names, identity aliases, dates and accessibility transcripts must not be cut to satisfy a numeric budget.

Every source paragraph is classified as required fact, helpful interpretation or optional history. Required facts move to an action, demonstration, comparison or short caption. Helpful interpretation is a replayable line or hint. Optional history remains in the journal under its discovered source.

A claim such as “the figure looks familiar” is observation; “Elian is safe” requires branch evidence. Keep this distinction in the script. Do not replace reading with an equally long unskippable monologue. Captions can be manually advanced; pressure pauses while required information is on screen.

Acceptance: an unfamiliar player can follow the minimal route without opening a lore document. Record their actual attempts and misunderstandings; word counts alone cannot pass this gate.
