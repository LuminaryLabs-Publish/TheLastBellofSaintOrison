# Journal Inventory and Evidence Presentation

**Target specification; runtime implementation pending.**

The journal holds discovered long-form writing, source images, recordings and concise summaries grouped by location. Entries identify whether they are observation, testimony, original proof or uncertain interpretation. Opening the journal is deliberate and pauses exposure under the shared rule.

New discoveries give a short unobtrusive confirmation and a replay action instead of forcing the journal open. Retain source passages verbatim where their evidentiary meaning matters. Names and aliases remain paired; unknowns do not fill themselves when a room is completed.

Inventory selection shows item identity, valid-use feedback and cancellation. Distinguish a permanent item from a local prop and an original from a copy. The evidence tray at Town Square uses the actual held records, never a fixed visual set that implies absent proof exists.

The journal and satchel project owner state. They must not independently grant clues, combine items or rewrite past choices. Reopening a record is read-only except for optional UI history.
