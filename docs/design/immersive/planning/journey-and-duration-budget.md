# Journey and Duration Budget

**Specification package; no runtime or Drive changes.**

## Estimate, not proof
This collection has 35 required encounters and 10 optional encounters. At an unmeasured nominal two active minutes each, required interaction is 70 minutes; including all optional encounters adds 20. These figures count one chosen outcome per decision, not every alternative. Each encounter currently has a 1–3 minute design range and no measured time.

The source game allocates 180 minutes across rooms. That allocation is not evidence that the redesigned actions fill 180 minutes. Required actions alone leave 110 minutes unexplained at the nominal estimate; even all encounters leave 90. Do not fill the gap with waiting, repeated notes or forced exposition.

## Measurement worksheet
For each room record active control time, search/observation time, dialogue time, traversal time, recovery time, optional lore time and paused accessibility time. Mark overlapping activities; audio during walking is counted once. Record route, prior familiarity, settings and participant, then median/range from actual sessions. Keep assistance pauses separate from active content duration without treating assisted play as invalid.

## Before a three-hour claim
Build the encounters, measure first-time routes and identify sections that feel rushed, repetitive or empty. Add meaningful story interactions only when that review identifies a concrete need. The game can become shorter when mandatory reading is removed; that is an honest design tradeoff to evaluate, not a reason to inflate document estimates.
