# Revised Story Treatment

**Target design specification; not implemented gameplay.**

Mara follows Elian’s cassette into Saint Orison to find her sister. At the gate she observes how to cross and learns to use an intentionally blank record rather than offer a living name. The centre and café establish Elian’s route and voluntary attempt to interrupt the relay through matching physical and recorded evidence.

The market and apothecary make preparation tangible: secure the crossing, collect the service lantern, test resin and learn that protection cannot make a voice truthful. The tower makes the choice between preserving a useful channel and breaking it physically explicit. School and Archive require comparison of independent records rather than belief in an authoritative voice. Four identities remain paired with aliases; the oxygen shutoff at 02:13 remains a deliberate human act supported by recoverable original evidence.

At the chapel Mara verifies Elian’s phrase and actual place. Elian either joins her through a real stair or maintains a protected communication station. The parish and tunnels connect family responsibility, a relay contract and practical descent equipment to the separate vessel that holds erased patterns.

At the Well, four more paired identities make eight verified names. An isolated model lets the player inspect Witness, Silence and Vessel before committing one. The return demonstrates consequences without confiscating evidence or inventing an unreachable escape. Square and Road give the player room to distinguish proof from uncertainty, distribute records and leave or remain briefly to finish copying them.

This treatment replaces mandatory explanatory panels with authored action, short speech and inspectable physical relationships. It does not establish three hours of content, final atmosphere or a new ending. Those require implementation and player review.
