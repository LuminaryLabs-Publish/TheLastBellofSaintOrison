## Convergence and revisits
All 31 existing local departures retain their identity and lead to the next listed location. They are not 31 independent campaigns. Optional flags travel with their owners. Before the Well departure, the existing map permits revisits to entered rooms and recovery of missed evidence while keeping committed choices locked. A broken relay cannot be repaired by revisiting the Tower. After the Well intervention, the return runs forward through Ascent, Square and Road.

Entry → required encounter facts → logical primary completion → equipment gate → optional or mandatory departure choice → Scene transition. Do not equate the order of files with a runnable Sequence graph. Continuous pressure belongs to Threat, while finite encounter work coordinates bounded operations.

Town Gate establishes the blank identity rule and gives the intake key. Market supplies the lantern. Apothecary produces resin and combines protection. School verifies four names and the First Register. Archive offers original proof. Chapel resolves Elian’s actual location. Parish supplies contract and chalk. Tunnels demonstrate a vessel, not another child. Well verifies the second four names and commits the intervention. The final three rooms demonstrate and acknowledge consequences.
