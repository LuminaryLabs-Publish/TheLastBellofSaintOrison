## Player purpose

Secure the real shutter and take a service lantern.

## Entry and first impression

Arrive from closed-cafe. Complete MS-01 first.

A tethered pin hangs beside the counterweight; the lantern is inside arm’s reach of shelter.

## Objects and controls

- pin: seat in aligned notch
- winch: raise shutter
- safety-catch: engage
- lantern: collect

## Obtainable clues

- Pin, raised shutter and catch are embossed left to right on the freight plate.
- The supported notch from MS-01 is reproduced on the real counterweight.

## Exact action sequence

1. Seat the pin while the weight rests on its support.
2. Turn the winch until the opening clears.
3. Engage the catch; its pawl visibly carries the load.
4. Take the existing service lantern before leaving.

## Wrong attempts and assistance

The winch interlock refuses motion without the pin; lowering remains safe until the catch is engaged.

Hints progress only on request:

1. Support the weight before lifting.
2. A raised shutter still needs its catch.
3. Pin, raise, catch, take lantern.

## Completion

Shutter locked open and lantern in Inventory; departure requires both.
