# Market Street — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Secure the shutter and collect a service lantern.

Arrival from closed-cafe; departure to apothecary. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

A scale-model freight opening cycles behind a protective screen.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Cross the Shutter Cycle**. Understand how the freight shutter loads its counterweight. Controls: demo-crank, weight-window, stop-marker. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Release the Jammed Winch**. Secure the real shutter and take a service lantern. Controls: pin, winch, safety-catch, lantern. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Recover the Stranded Parcel**. Mark a recognizable return crossing. Controls: tether, parcel-flap, marker. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

Rain drums on canvas roofs. An abandoned freight lantern lies beside a half-lowered shutter. The café’s cable disappears through a service trunk on the far side. This crossing can be left safe for someone returning behind you.
