# Visitor Centre — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Restore the map and establish Elian’s next stop.

Arrival from town-gate; departure to closed-cafe. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

Three low-voltage toggles have circle, triangle and square plates; the map is dark.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Restore the Map Circuit**. Power the isolated map transformer. Controls: circle-isolator, triangle-feed, square-feed. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Release the District Key**. Expose the café route using the intake key already obtained. Controls: intake-lock, map-overlay, wave-marker. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Find the Altered Route**. Compare the official evacuation with its earlier copy. Controls: carbon-tab, map-layer, camera-mark. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

The visitor centre smells of warm paper. Every chair faces the illuminated town map. An evacuation notice has yesterday’s date, but the visitor book was signed this morning. Elian has drawn a small wave beside her entry. She did not leave by the road.

This paragraph is archival narrative context, not a required arrival popup in the target design. Deliver its essential facts through the encounter actions; retain full writing in the optional journal.

## Existing safe retreat

The service cabinet has a manual isolation switch. You turn it off and wait in the daylight panel by the door.
