## Purpose and geography

## First readable situation

Three dated photographs show four bricked window panels, alcove one and seven bell bands.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Open the Mechanical Safe**. Recover the relay contract from physical evidence in the room. Controls: photograph-slider, number-wheel, safe-handle. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Expose the Descent Entrance**. Find the utility stair and prepare a return route. Controls: cabinet-catch, survey-board, chalk, letter-view. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Examine the Hidden Photograph**. Give the family discovery a personal response. Controls: photo-flap, conversation, journal. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.
