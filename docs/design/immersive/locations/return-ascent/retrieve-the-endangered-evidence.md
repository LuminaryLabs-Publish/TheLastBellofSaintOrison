## Player purpose

Choose whether to spend time helping Elian secure her route and copies.

## Entry and first impression

Arrive from well-chamber. Complete RA-01 first. Also complete the second required encounter before departure; this optional encounter never supplies its prerequisites.

A duplicate record pouch hangs beside the emergency-line switch; Mara’s originals remain in her satchel.

## Objects and controls

- line-switch: hold or toggle assisted hold
- duplicate-pouch: pull onto shelf
- leave-control: skip optional assistance

## Obtainable clues

- The pouch is explicitly a duplicate; required evidence is never removed from Inventory.
- Elian’s confirmation comes from the station or stair matching the earlier choice.

## Exact action sequence

1. Preview optional assistance without threatening the safe upper exit.
2. Keep the line open and pull the duplicate pouch onto its shelf.
3. Wait for the matching route confirmation, then release the line; or skip and continue safely.

## Wrong attempts and assistance

Releasing early pauses assistance rather than erasing proof; the upper gate stays open.

Hints progress only on request:

1. Your originals are already safe.
2. The line helps confirm another person’s route.
3. Hold the line until the open-eye confirmation, or leave.

## Completion

Optional elianHelped recorded; no retroactive change to the already-committed ending.

## Supported alternatives

The exact sequence above is the supported logical solution. Pointer, keyboard and assisted controls are equivalent input paths, not alternate story outcomes. Skipping is supported and retains all mandatory progression.

## Pressure and accessible operation

No decision countdown. Pause threat while options or their required consequences are being presented; require a separate confirmation for irreversible action. At 50% show the room warning with a stable warning icon: “Water strikes the pump housing from the wrong side.” At 80% show its critical caption and distinct double-border marker: “The upper service gate begins to settle into its frame.” At the room limit release held controls safely and use the established retreat: You shelter in the pump niche. Its mechanical hand release still works after the bell’s intervention. Never destroy required evidence. An optional detour may cost active time, but skipping it changes no required gate. Menus, hints, required captions/consequence explanations and user-requested journal reading pause exposure; normal close inspection keeps the world active unless a required explanation is on screen. Source allocations are tuning starting points, not measured encounter durations. Passive warning icons and ambient captions do not pause time; only an explicitly opened required explanation does. After a voluntary choice, active physical execution may resume room pressure; preview and confirmation never have a deadline.

Controls line-switch, duplicate-pouch, leave-control must each have a focusable name and a visible response. Pointer drag/rotation has discrete click controls and keyboard previous/next/confirm equivalents. Holds offer press-to-latch and press-to-release assistance; release on blur, pause or scene exit cannot commit a destructive action. Caption all significant sounds; preserve symbol or shape distinctions independently of color. Replay demonstrations by user-stepped stages when timing is assisted. Keep clue text at supported large scale with paging inside the inspection area, and retain a stable back/exit action. Reduced motion replaces continuous movement with labelled state changes while preserving clue order.

## Save, reset and cancellation

Proposed Investigation-owned logical record: encounterId=RA-03, definitionVersion=1, confirmedStep (0 through 3), completed, and a validated control-value record keyed only by line-switch, duplicate-pouch, leave-control. Decision records additionally store a preview selection separately from the single Narrative/Campaign commit. This is a proposed schema extension, not an existing installed API. Snapshot logical state plus owning-domain effects at one safe commit boundary. Never save DOM nodes, callbacks, drag pointers, audio handles, frame timestamps or an animation position as authority. On restore, reconstruct controls from confirmed state and replay only noncommitting feedback; never award a reward again. An interrupted unconfirmed drag/hold returns to its last stable detent. A wrong attempt resets only the local unsolved control sequence specified above; verified clues, items, primary completion and committed choices remain. Validate the whole snapshot and definition version before replacing any live owner; an unknown version leaves the current session unchanged. Entry/exit removes input subscriptions, stops local audio and cancels uncommitted operation receipts. Retry preserves original branch commitments and can always return to the named shelter.
