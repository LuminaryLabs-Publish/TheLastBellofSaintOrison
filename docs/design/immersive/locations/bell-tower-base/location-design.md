# Bell Tower Base — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Learn the bell cycle and choose how to interrupt it.

Arrival from apothecary; departure to school-hall. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

An exposed gauge shows tension feeding the governor; bypass, tension and brake have distinct shapes.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Balance the Governor**. Delay a bell cycle without damaging the relay. Controls: bypass-wheel, tension-lever, brake-handle. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Isolate the Access Mechanism**. Learn the core isolation method before reaching the chamber. Controls: training-cover, brace-pads, access-wheel. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Preserve or Disable the Relay**. Choose whether the public transmission channel survives. Controls: intact-route, break-guard, confirm-control. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

A huge shaft turns above the maintenance platform. Each revolution pulls a cable into the hospital below. The governor can delay one cycle without breaking the transmission line. A crowbar offers a faster, permanent silence for this local mechanism.
