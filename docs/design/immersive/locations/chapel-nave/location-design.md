# Chapel Nave — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Verify Elian’s identity and decide whether to release her.

Arrival from flooded-archive; departure to parish-house. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

The annex window and labelled cable both lead to a visible protected station beside the chapel.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Restore the Intercom Connection**. Reach the person in the physical annex. Controls: patch-cord, call-switch, window. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Verify the Survivor’s Account**. Compare a private response with the original cassette. Controls: cassette, intercom, comparison-card. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Release or Leave the Survivor**. Choose whether Elian travels physically or maintains the protected line. Controls: stair-preview, line-preview, confirm. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

Father Vale waits beside a closed side chapel. He says Elian is safe because she is contained. Her voice arrives through a hospital intercom behind him: “Ask me something he did not teach me.” The private phrase on your cassette offers a better test than the face in the dark.

This paragraph is archival narrative context, not a required arrival popup in the target design. Deliver its essential facts through the encounter actions; retain full writing in the optional journal.

## Existing safe retreat

You switch to the local line and close the public microphone. Elian waits without repeating your name.
