# Well Chamber — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Recover the Bell Core and choose a fully explained intervention.

Arrival from maintenance-tunnels; departure to return-ascent. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

The platform has a support pawl and the core has shutter, feed and collar controls.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Stabilize the Inspection Platform**. Reach and safely release the resonator. Controls: support-pawl, acoustic-shutter, feed, collars. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Trace the Bell Mechanism**. Understand the evidence and three interventions before choosing. Controls: record-pairs, method-model, privacy-cover. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Commit the Final Intervention**. Choose Witness, Silence or Vessel with informed consent. Controls: witness-console, silence-brace, self-pattern, confirm. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

The empty thirteenth channel is the vessel.
