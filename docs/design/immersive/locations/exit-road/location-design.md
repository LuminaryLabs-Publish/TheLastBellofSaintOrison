# Exit Road — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Acknowledge the consequence and decide what you carry home.

Arrival from town-square; departure to the committed ending and credits. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

The road repeats its opening sightline; the cassette and distant bell behave according to the ending.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Inspect the Final Trace**. Acknowledge what changed before leaving. Controls: cassette, road-view, record-envelope. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Keep or Leave the Recording**. Choose how to preserve the recording without destroying its history. Controls: satchel-slot, copy-envelope, first-line. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Leave or Remain**. Finish the journey through an ordinary responsibility. Controls: road, copying-bench, confirm. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

The gatehouse fan turns in the morning air. The fourth roster line is still blank unless you offered your pattern below. You stop where the cassette first played. The sea sounds larger now that nothing is trying to speak over it.

This paragraph is archival narrative context, not a required arrival popup in the target design. Deliver its essential facts through the encounter actions; retain full writing in the optional journal.
