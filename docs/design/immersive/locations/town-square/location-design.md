# Town Square — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Choose how to share the record without claiming what you cannot prove.

Arrival from return-ascent; departure to exit-road. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

The public stand, relay indicator and residents reflect the committed method.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Read the Town’s Changes**. Distinguish observed consequences from an assumed happy ending. Controls: relay-indicator, notice-stand, evidence-tray. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Assemble the Account**. Prepare an honest account without claiming missing proof. Controls: record-cards, unknown-card, prepare-control. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Share or Withhold the Evidence**. Choose custody while respecting the already-committed ending. Controls: copy-station, custody-envelope, private-margin. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

The town square is quiet enough to hear the sea. A few residents wait beside the public notice stand. They ask what happened beneath their homes. You have knowledge, physical evidence and gaps. None of those should be disguised as another.

This paragraph is archival narrative context, not a required arrival popup in the target design. Deliver its essential facts through the encounter actions; retain full writing in the optional journal.

## Existing safe retreat

You sit on the dry edge of the fountain and let the quiet remain quiet.
