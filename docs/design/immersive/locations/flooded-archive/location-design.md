# Flooded Archive — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Recover the shutoff record and its carbon original.

Arrival from school-hall; departure to chapel-nave. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

Two discharge pipes lead visibly to a street grate and to the records sump.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Restore the Pump Flow**. Drain a working aisle without flooding the records sump. Controls: discharge-valve, hand-pump, gauge. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Locate the Original Record**. Find the deliberate oxygen shutoff record. Controls: department-wheel, equipment-wheel, time-wheel, drawer. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Recover the Handwritten Correction**. Choose whether to retrieve stronger original proof. Controls: tether, case-clasp, leave-route. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

Water presses paper against the lower shelves. The index desk is raised above the flood. A pump can expose the correct aisle briefly, but searching every cabinet would lose the record you actually need. You begin with the index, not the water.

## Existing safe retreat
