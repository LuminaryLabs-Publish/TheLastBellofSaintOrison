## Player purpose

Drain a working aisle without flooding the records sump.

## Entry and first impression

Arrive from school-hall. This encounter can be examined immediately; commits follow its stated prerequisites.

Two discharge pipes lead visibly to a street grate and to the records sump.

## Objects and controls

- discharge-valve: choose street drain
- hand-pump: operate or toggle assisted pumping
- gauge: inspect notched water marks

## Obtainable clues

- A surface float moves toward the street grate on the safe test route.
- The records-sump pipe ends beside vulnerable shelves.

## Exact action sequence

1. Trace both pipes from the dry raised desk.
2. Set discharge to the street grate and operate the hand pump.
3. Observe the aisle clear to the lower notched mark; keep the raised walkway available.

## Wrong attempts and assistance

Wrong discharge closes a test flap before flooding shelves; return the valve to neutral and retry.

Hints progress only on request:

1. Trace where the pipe ends.
2. Send water away from the records.
3. Street drain, then pump.

## Completion

archive-safe learned and a working aisle drained; pressure cannot destroy essential records.
