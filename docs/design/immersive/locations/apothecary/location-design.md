# Apothecary — Location Design

**Target specification; existing playable content is unchanged.**

## Purpose and geography

Test the resin, then prepare the lantern.

Arrival from market-street; departure to bell-tower-base. Reuse the current authored environment and entry/exit identities. New controls occupy reachable close-inspection views rather than requiring new locomotion.

## First readable situation

Three sample wells have round/red, square/amber and triangular/blue seals.

## Authored interaction zones

- Zone 1: **Test the Protective Mixture**. Identify the resin that burns without smoke. Controls: sample-spoon, test-burner, sample-selector. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 2: **Prepare the Lantern Wick**. Treat the service lantern and understand its limits. Controls: reservoir, tested-resin, wick-wheel, alcove-shutter. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.
- Zone 3: **Examine the Failed Batch**. Separate evidence of human neglect from the supernatural effect. Controls: drawer, labels, clinical-note. A wide view must expose its location before a close view is required.

## Spatial constraints

Keep the exit and named shelter visible from the primary interaction zone. No required control may sit behind a permanently locked optional object. Reused return environments keep their landmark positions; state variants change lighting and access, not the meaning of established navigation symbols. Initial blockout uses labelled zones; final dimensions and camera framing need a measured asset/layout review.

## Existing atmosphere to retain

Bottles crowd the window, turning streetlight green. The dispensing bench holds three samples and an intact test lamp. A handwritten warning separates two ideas: a flame can protect your breathing, but it cannot protect your judgment.

This paragraph is archival narrative context, not a required arrival popup in the target design. Deliver its essential facts through the encounter actions; retain full writing in the optional journal.

## Existing safe retreat

You close the fire screen and return to the ventilated dispensing window.
