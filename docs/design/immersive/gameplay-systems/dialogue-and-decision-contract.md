# Dialogue and Decision Contract

**Target specification; runtime implementation pending.**

Narrative owns passage progress, choices and relationship facts. Campaign owns the committed ending. Scene owns the actual location. A dialogue event cannot teleport Elian or override a Campaign decision.

Each short passage specifies speaker, source of knowledge, branch condition, interruption rule and equivalent caption. The original cassette and private phrase remain available without optional side content. Long letters and explanations are journal depth, not mandatory barriers after every click.

Choices have preview and commit states. Previewing shows the exact consequence, requirements and available cancellation. Irreversible choices require a second deliberate action with pressure paused. A repeated commit returns the previous result; a contradictory late commit is rejected. Endings and the broken relay must retain the existing guards.

Physical Elian appears only on the released path or after confirmed travel. Remote Elian remains at her station until a valid physical return is described. Trust does not automatically prove location or grant forgiveness. Later custody choices cannot undo Witness transmission or create a fourth ending.
