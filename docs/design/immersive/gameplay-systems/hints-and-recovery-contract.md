# Hints and Recovery Contract

**Target specification; runtime implementation pending.**

Each encounter has three requested hints: draw attention to evidence, explain a relevant relationship, then provide the actionable solution. Hints do not auto-solve or secretly deduct a scarce item. Do not repeat the same vague instruction at every level.

The first wrong attempt should expose why it failed: blocked pin hole, mismatched seal, wrong radio trace or loaded interlock. Controls remain inspectable and the correct state remains reachable. Essential documents stay in protective cases; required tools cannot fall into inaccessible space.

Recovery returns to a documented stable state and shelter. Preserve completed clues, rewards and irreversible decisions. Restarting an unsolved local mechanism must not reset the campaign. A player who skips optional evidence can still reach at least an eligible ending; a player who deliberately breaks Witness prerequisites receives an explicit warning.

Test unfamiliar-player comprehension separately from automated path validity. If players cannot identify the cause of a failure, revise its feedback before reducing the puzzle to a paragraph of instructions.
